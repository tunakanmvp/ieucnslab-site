Two series from the 3Blue1Brown channel explain the foundations of maths through visual intuition before formulas: [Essence of Linear Algebra](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/linear-algebra) and [Essence of Calculus](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/calculus).

**What's in it?** The linear algebra series covers vectors, matrices as linear transformations, the determinant, the dot product, change of basis, and eigenvectors and eigenvalues. The calculus series covers what a derivative is, the chain rule, Euler's number e, limits, integration and the fundamental theorem of calculus, and Taylor series.

**Why we recommend it:** Made up of 10 to 20-minute videos, these series are ideal to watch before, or alongside, Strang's linear algebra course or MIT's calculus courses. The view of "a matrix as a transformation" in particular makes many signal processing concepts click into place at once.

**Who is it for?** High-school maths is enough. Anyone who has met these topics before but never quite settled the "why".
