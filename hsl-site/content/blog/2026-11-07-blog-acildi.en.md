Our publications describe the results of our work, but rarely the path that led there. This blog is where we want to share that path: how an experiment was designed, the problems we ran into while recording, and why an analysis method did or didn't work.

Posts will be short and will come from different members of the team. If you'd like to ask a question or work with us, you can always get in touch.
