---
title: Music and the brain
weight: 30
summary: We study how the brain responds while listening to makam taksim improvisations.
---
We record EEG from participants listening to instrumental taksim recordings from Turkish makam music and compare the emotional and neural effects of different makams. Our sonification work, which turns EEG into sound and images, is part of this topic too.
