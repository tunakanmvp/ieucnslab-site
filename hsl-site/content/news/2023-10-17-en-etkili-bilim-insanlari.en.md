Four professors from Izmir University of Economics were included in the **"World's Most Influential Scientists"** list prepared by a research team led by Dr. John Ioannidis of Stanford University. Our lab's principal investigator, **Prof. Dr. Aydın Akan**, is among them. The list is compiled in two categories, career-long and single-year, using criteria such as publication and citation counts, authorship position and h-index.

The other IUE professors on the list were Prof. Dr. Levent Eren, Prof. Dr. Türker İnce and Prof. Dr. Murat Bengisu.
