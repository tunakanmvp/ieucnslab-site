Our lab has been accepted into the COST Action **EEG101: Fundamentals of Open & Rigorous EEG Science** ([CA24148](https://www.cost.eu/actions/CA24148/)).

EEG101 is a European research network launched 101 years after the discovery of the EEG signal. It aims to standardise recording, analysis and reporting methods that vary from lab to lab, bring large datasets together and make EEG research more reproducible. To do so, the network develops shared tools and protocols, runs summer schools and supports short research visits between labs.

Joining the network is an important opportunity for us to collaborate with EEG labs across Europe and to align our work with international standards.
