## Funding calls and research announcements

Last checked: **6 November 2026**

### Open calls

- **TÜBİTAK 2209-A and 2209-B.** Research and industry-oriented project support for associate and undergraduate students. Online applications close **11 November 2026 at 17:30**; institutional e-signatures are due **23 November 2026**. [Official call](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/duyuru/2209-universite-ogrencileri-arastirma-projeleri-destekleme-programi-ve-2209-b-universite-ogrencileri-sanayiye-yonelik-arastirma-projeleri-destegi-programi-2026-yili-cagrilari-acildi)
- **İZKA 2026 Technical Support.** Applications for the Green Transformation in Industry and İzmir Studies programmes close on KAYS on **30 December 2026**. Eligibility and scope vary by programme. [Official call and guides](https://izka.org.tr/2026-yili-teknik-destek-programlari-ilan-edildi/)
- **Horizon Europe 2027 Health Cluster.** Published topics include AI-based predictive biomarkers and clinical trials for neurodegenerative diseases. HaDEA lists **17 February 2027 at 17:00 Brussels time** as the first submission deadline. Check the schedule on the specific topic page before applying. [HaDEA notice and topic links](https://hadea.ec.europa.eu/news/2027-horizon-europe-health-calls-are-open-proposals-2026-10-29_en)

### IUE announcements

- **IUE Scientific Research Projects (BAP).** Eligible IUE researchers submit their internal funding application form to the Project Development and Technology Transfer Office. [IUE BAP and TTO information](https://ieutto.izmirekonomi.edu.tr/sss/)
- **Faculty of Engineering FENG 497.** The Fall 2026–2027 graduation project proposal deadline is **13 November 2026**. [IUE project calendar](https://fecs.ieu.edu.tr/tr/feng-497-graduation-project)

### Events and second-stage submissions

- **Horizon Europe Health Cluster.** Online brokerage event on **24–25 November 2026** and Health Cluster Info Day on **26 November 2026**. [Event and registration information](https://ufukavrupa.org.tr/tr/haberler/ufuk-avrupa-saglik-kumesi-guncel-cagrilar-ve-etkinlik-bilgileri)
