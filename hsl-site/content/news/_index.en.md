## Funding Calls And Research Announcements

Last checked: **9 November 2026**

### Open Calls

- **TÜBİTAK 2209-A and 2209-B.** Research and industry-oriented project support for associate and undergraduate students. Online applications close **11 November 2026 at 17:30**; institutional e-signatures are due **23 November 2026**. [Official call](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/duyuru/2209-universite-ogrencileri-arastirma-projeleri-destekleme-programi-ve-2209-b-universite-ogrencileri-sanayiye-yonelik-arastirma-projeleri-destegi-programi-2026-yili-cagrilari-acildi)
- **TÜBİTAK 2247-C STAR Undergraduate.** Undergraduate students can apply to research projects recruiting fellows by **16 November 2026 at 17:30 (Türkiye time)**. [Official call and application link](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/burslar/lisans-onlisans/burs-programlari/2247-c-stajyer-arastirmaci-burs-programi-star)
- **TÜBİTAK TEYDEB 1507 and 1707.** The 1507 call supports R&D projects by Türkiye-based SMEs incorporated as capital companies. Organisation preregistration closes **23 November** and proposal submission closes **25 November 2026**. The 1707 call supports joint R&D projects by a customer organisation and an SME supplier. Organisation preregistration closes **25 November** and proposal submission closes **27 November 2026**. [Updated official schedule](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/duyuru/1507-kobi-ar-ge-baslangic-destek-programi-ve-1707-siparise-dayali-ar-ge-projeleri-icin-kobi-destekleme-programi-2026-yili-cagrilarinin-suresi-uzatildi) · [PRODİS](https://eteydeb.tubitak.gov.tr/)
- **TÜBİTAK 2247-A and 2247-D.** For experienced and early-career researchers looking to build or develop a research team. Research experience and other eligibility criteria differ by programme. First-stage applications close **15 December 2026 at 17:30 (Türkiye time)**. [Official call and application link](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/duyuru/2247-ulusal-lider-arastirmacilar-programi-ve-2247-d-ulusal-genc-liderler-programi-2026-yili-cagrilari-acildi)
- **TÜBİTAK–ARIS 2508.** For joint research teams from Türkiye and Slovenia. The call covers health and information and communication technologies, among other fields. The Türkiye submission deadline is **21 December 2026 at 23:59**, and e-signatures are due **28 December 2026 at 23:59** (Türkiye time). [Official call and application link](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/duyuru/2508-tubitak-slovenya-arastirma-ve-yenilik-ajansi-aris-ikili-birligi-programi-2026-yili-cagrisi-acildi)
- **TÜBİTAK 2244 Industrial PhD Programme.** For doctoral training projects run jointly by universities and industry partners. Applications close **22 December 2026**. [Official call and application link](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/duyuru/2244-sanayi-doktora-programi-2026-yili-cagrisi-acildi-0)
- **İZKA 2026 Technical Support.** Applications for the Green Transformation in Industry and İzmir Studies programmes close on KAYS on **30 December 2026**. Eligibility and scope vary by programme. [Official call and guides](https://izka.org.tr/2026-yili-teknik-destek-programlari-ilan-edildi/)
- **Horizon Europe 2027 Health Cluster.** Published topics include AI-based predictive biomarkers and clinical trials for neurodegenerative diseases. HaDEA lists **17 February 2027 at 17:00 Brussels time** as the first submission deadline. Check the schedule on the specific topic page before applying. [HaDEA notice and topic links](https://hadea.ec.europa.eu/news/2027-horizon-europe-health-calls-are-open-proposals-2026-10-29_en)
- **ERA4Health IICS4all 2027.** For international consortia conducting pragmatic phase III clinical trials in cardiovascular, autoimmune, metabolic or noncommunicable respiratory diseases. Partners in Türkiye must meet TÜSEB’s national eligibility rules. Pre-proposals are due **14 January 2027 at 14:00 (Türkiye time)**. [TÜSEB notice and eligibility](https://www.tuseb.gov.tr/public/news/era4health-partnership-2027-investigator-initiated-clinical-studies-iics4all-international-joint-call-is-open-for-applications-20261106) · [Submission portal](https://ptoutline.eu/app/IICS4all)
