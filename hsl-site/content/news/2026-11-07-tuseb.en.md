Our proposal to the 2026 group B call of the Health Institutes of Türkiye (TÜSEB) has passed the **pre-evaluation** stage.

In the project we are working on an improved **fNIRS** device. fNIRS (functional near-infrared spectroscopy) is a portable brain imaging method that measures changes in blood oxygenation in the brain using harmless near-infrared light sent through the scalp. Our goal is to improve recording quality and coverage with AI running on the device itself.

The detailed evaluation result will be announced on **18 December 2026**. We'll share it here.
