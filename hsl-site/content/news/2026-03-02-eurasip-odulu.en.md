Our lab's principal investigator and Head of the IUE Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering, **Prof. Dr. Aydın Akan**, has received the **2026 Meritorious Service Award** of the European Association for Signal Processing (**EURASIP**). Given since 2000, the award goes to only one person each year. It will be presented at a ceremony in Bruges, Belgium, between 31 August and 4 September 2026.

Akan has worked in signal processing since the 1990s and previously chaired the EURASIP Technical Area Committee on Biomedical Image and Signal Analytics. Of his two textbooks, *Signals and Systems Using MATLAB* is used in courses at many universities. Over a career of about 30 years he has supervised 38 PhD and 57 master's graduates.

Congratulations to our professor.
