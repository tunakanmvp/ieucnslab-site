In a neuromarketing study carried out in our lab, we examined how sending online orders in **scented or unscented packaging** affects consumers. In the first study of its kind in Türkiye, we recorded participants' brain signals with **EEG** and their arousal with **skin conductance**.

Products sent with a scent increased participants' pleasure and arousal responses by about **90%** compared with unscented products. The physiological results agreed with participants' questionnaire responses **92%** of the time. In a follow-up, we repeated the experiment with four different scents and found that the firm using the most-liked scent became more likely to be preferred.

![A moment from the study](https://phoenix.ieu.edu.tr/betanix/images/uploads/haber/9615_2.jpeg "Image: Izmir University of Economics")
