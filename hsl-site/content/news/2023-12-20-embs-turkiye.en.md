Our lab's principal investigator, **Prof. Dr. Aydın Akan**, has been elected **chair of the Türkiye chapter** of the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society (**EMBS**). Elected by the votes of EMBS Türkiye members for a three-year term, Akan aims to organise national and international events in biomedical engineering and training for students.

His work covers the detection of conditions such as epilepsy, dementia and attention deficit using EEG signal processing and artificial intelligence, as well as neuromarketing applications.
